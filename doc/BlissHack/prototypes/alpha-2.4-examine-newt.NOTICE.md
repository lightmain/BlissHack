# Alpha-2.4 Newt Examine Prototype: Content Notice

## NetHackWiki excerpts

- Title: "Newt"
- Authors: NetHackWiki contributors
- Fixed revision: 205110
- Source: https://nethackwiki.com/index.php?title=Newt&oldid=205110
- Revision history: https://nethackwiki.com/index.php?title=Newt&action=history
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported
- License URI: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

The prototype selects complete paragraphs from the fixed revision. It does
not summarize, translate, rewrite, or splice those paragraphs. Presentation
changes are limited to omitting Wiki markup and internal link destinations;
the displayed wording is unchanged.

Origin material, encyclopedia quotations, images, game messages, and other
content with separate or uncertain provenance are not included as
NetHackWiki excerpts.

## NetHack data

Monster statistics, attacks, traits, generation flags, corpse weight, and
nutrition are represented from `include/monsters.h` in the NetHack 5.0 source
tree. NetHack licensing terms are in `dat/license`.

The prototype keeps NetHack-derived facts and NetHackWiki excerpts in
separately marked regions so their provenance and licenses are not conflated.
